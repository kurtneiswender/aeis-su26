§ 01

What You Are Submitting.

Act III closes with a two-part Canvas submission: a PDF package containing your lighting plan, lumen-method calculations, illuminance analysis, luminaire schedule, electrical load and panel work, energy figures, and pin-up slides; and a pasted text entry of your Decision Log entries from Weeks 6–8. Both parts are required. A missing Decision Log text entry will score zero on Criterion 5 regardless of the quality of the PDF.

The PDF is graded against a five-criterion rubric worth 20 points total. The central question for this act: does the hall light as a set of layered systems that hit their targets — and does the electricity behind them hold together? Each space must reach its IES illuminance target by calculation and simulation. Every luminaire must be a real product backed by photometric data. The load, panel schedule, LPD figure, and PV size must be consistent with the fixtures you actually specified.

LightStanza (or Revit's lighting analysis) is the required illuminance tool for this act. Results must appear as exported false-color maps or screenshots, not as verbal descriptions of what the software showed.

§ 02

PDF Package Contents.

Export everything into a single PDF. Page order does not need to follow this list exactly, but all items must be present. Label each page or section clearly.

Lighting Plan / Reflected Ceiling Plan

Plan (and RCP where it reads better) showing luminaire locations for the hall and prefunction, with the four lighting layers distinguished — house, egress/aisle, work/rehearsal, and architectural accent — and the control zone each is on. Include a fixture-type legend keyed to your schedule. North arrow required. Minimum 1/8″ = 1′ scale or equivalent.

Lumen-Method Calculations

The hand calculation for your key spaces — at minimum the house and the prefunction, plus stage/work light if applicable. For each: the maintained target E (fc + lux) with the IES Lighting Handbook category cited; delivered lumens Φ, CU, and LLF with a one-line justification each; N = (E·A)/(Φ·CU·LLF) solved, then N plugged back to report the achieved E; and the installed watts (N×W) for the space.

Illuminance Analysis — LightStanza / Revit

False-color illuminance maps (footcandles or lux) for your primary spaces, exported from LightStanza or Revit. Annotate each map with the target level and the achieved average, and note whether the simulation agrees with your lumen-method hand calc within reason. If it does not, identify the most likely cause (CU assumption, reflectances, fixture spacing, surface losses).

Luminaire Schedule

A schedule of every fixture type, each a real specified product backed by an IES file. For each type list: delivered (fixture) lumens Φ, input watts, CCT, CRI, distribution type, and mounting. Tag each type to the layer and task it serves (e.g., warm wide-diffuse house downlight; high-CRI accent on stage; narrow direct over aisles; asymmetric cove wash). Use delivered lumens, not lamp lumens.

Electrical Load Summary + Panel Schedule

The connected lighting load summed by space and by circuit (W and VA / amps), grouped into logical circuits (by layer, zone, or scene). A panel schedule listing each circuit with its load, breaker size, and phase, plus the total connected load and a reasonable demand. Acknowledge other building loads (receptacles, mechanical) with allowances even if not fully detailed.

LPD Compliance Check

Installed Lighting Power Density (W/ft²) for the hall and/or whole building, compared explicitly to the ASHRAE 90.1 / IECC allowance for the space type, with a clear pass/fail. If you are over the allowance, state the design response (higher-efficacy fixtures, fewer fixtures, or a controls credit).

PV Sizing Calculation

Size a rooftop PV array to offset a stated fraction of the building's lighting (or total) load. Show the target offset, the array size in kW, the module count, and the approximate roof area required, with your assumptions noted (module wattage, local peak sun-hours). A back-of-envelope sizing is expected here, not a full production model.

Act III Pin-Up Slides

Your presentation from the Week 9 in-class review, exported as PDF pages. Graded on content, not slide design. A concise 6–8 slides covering: the layered lighting concept, illuminance results vs. targets, the luminaire schedule, the LPD/PV energy story, and the one cross-system trade-off you navigated (where lighting met an Act I acoustic surface or your Act II daylight strategy).

§ 04

Rubric — 20 Points.

Five criteria, four points each, on a five-level scale: Exceeds (4) · Mastery (3) · Near (2) · Below (1) · No Evidence (0). Scores map directly to the gradebook: 20 raw points = 10% of the course grade. Each criterion is evaluated independently. Mastery represents full competency at the graduate level for this stage of the project.

01   Lighting Design & Illuminance

SC.4B SC.4E 4 pts
Exceeds4
Lumen-method calculations are shown for the key spaces (house, prefunction, and stage/work light), each with a cited IES maintained target (fc + lux) and the achieved E reported. Results are verified against a LightStanza or Revit false-color illuminance map; any discrepancy between hand calc and simulation is explained. A reflected ceiling or lighting plan shows the four layers — house, egress, work, accent — each on its own control zone, with fixture locations and a legend. The plan reads as a designed system, not a uniform grid.
Mastery3
Lumen-method calcs are present for at least two spaces with targets cited and achieved E reported. An illuminance map is included. The lighting plan shows the layers and controls with minor gaps — one layer or control zone is unclear, or simulation and hand calc are not directly compared.
Near2
A single calc or a generic illuminance map without comparison to a target. The plan shows fixtures but does not differentiate the layers or their controls. Lighting is treated as one level for the whole room.
Below1
No lumen-method calculation, or no lighting plan. Fixtures are placed without a target illuminance or any analysis to support the layout.
No Evidence0
Nothing submitted addressing lighting levels or layout.

02   Luminaire Selection & Photometric Logic

SC.4B 4 pts
Exceeds4
A luminaire schedule lists real, specified fixtures backed by IES files. Each row gives delivered (fixture) lumens Φ, input watts, CCT, CRI, distribution type, and mounting. Every fixture is matched to its job: narrow/direct over aisles and apron; wide/diffuse warm light for the house and lobby; high-CRI (≥ 90) accent on the stage; asymmetric wash on architectural surfaces. Delivered lumens — not lamp lumens — are used in the calculations.
Mastery3
Schedule lists at least three real fixtures with most specifications present. Distribution, CCT, and CRI are mostly matched to task. Delivered lumens are used. Minor gaps — one fixture missing a spec, or one task/distribution mismatch unaddressed.
Near2
Fixtures are named but the schedule is incomplete (no IES data, or CCT/CRI/distribution not stated). Matching of fixture to task is weak or asserted without reasoning.
Below1
Generic fixtures with no specifications and no schedule. No photometric data of any kind.
No Evidence0
No luminaire selection submitted.

03   Electrical Distribution & Load

SC.4E 4 pts
Exceeds4
Connected lighting load is summed by space and by circuit (W and VA/A). Circuits are grouped by a logical principle — by layer, zone, or control scene — and the grouping is explained. A panel schedule lists each circuit with its load, breaker size, and phase, and reports the total connected load and a reasonable demand. Other building loads (receptacles, mechanical) are acknowledged with allowances even if not fully detailed.
Mastery3
A load summary and a panel schedule are present with minor gaps — breaker sizing is approximate, one zone is missing, or demand factors are not applied. Circuit grouping is reasonable.
Near2
A partial load list or a circuiting diagram, but no organized panel schedule. Loads are not totaled, or grouping logic is absent.
Below1
Loads are mentioned without calculation, or no distribution work beyond naming a panel.
No Evidence0
No electrical distribution work submitted.

04   Energy Performance — LPD & PV

SC.4B SC.4E 4 pts
Exceeds4
