## How to Use This Document

Add a new entry each time you make a significant design decision. Aim for **2–4 entries per week.** Do not delete or rewrite earlier entries — your reasoning will evolve across the term, and that evolution is the point. If you change your mind, make a new entry that references the earlier one.

Submit this document link to Canvas once in **Week 1.** The instructor has view and comment access for the full term. The log is reviewed formally at:

- **Week 3** — Act I Close (Acoustics)
- **Week 5** — Act II Close (Daylighting)
- **Week 8** — Act III Close (Lighting + Electrical)
- **Week 10** — Encore (Final Presentation)

### Entry Fields

| Field | What goes here |
|---|---|
| **Decision** | What you chose or committed to |
| **System** | Acoustics · Daylighting · Lighting · Electrical · Integration |
| **Space** | House · Stage · Prefunction · Envelope · Multiple |
| **Reasoning** | Why — connect to calculations, readings, or design intent. A gut response is fine in Week 1; by Week 3 it needs numbers. |
| **Trade-off** | What you gave up, or what constraint shaped the decision |
| **Reconsider** | What would cause you to revisit this decision later |
