## Criterion 1 — Acoustic Geometry
**NAAB SC.4B** · *4 points*

> Does the plan and section demonstrate that room shape was driven by acoustic intent — volume, proportions, surface angles, early reflection paths?

| Rating | Points | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds** | 4 | Plan and section clearly show acoustic intent: volume is calculated and appropriate for the RT60 target; surface angles are drawn to direct early reflections toward listeners; geometry decisions are explicitly connected to acoustic principles in the submission. |
| **Mastery** | 3 | Geometry shows awareness of acoustic principles with minor gaps. Volume is approximately correct; some surface angles are considered; connection to acoustic intent is present but not fully developed in all areas. |
| **Near** | 2 | Geometry is largely borrowed from the Week 1 starter without acoustic development. Volume is not calculated or is significantly off-target. Surface treatment is applied without explanation of acoustic role. |
| **Below** | 1 | Submitted plan/section shows no evidence that acoustic principles influenced shape. Room is a default box with no sectional response to sound. |
| **No Evidence** | 0 | No plan or section submitted. Nothing in the package shows the room's shape. |

## Criterion 2 — Quantitative Analysis (RT60)
**NAAB SC.4B + SC.4E** · *4 points*

> Are the RT60 calculations correct, complete, and calibrated against an appropriate target? Does the student use both hand calculation and simulation (Pachyderm), and do they account for the program (speech vs. music)?

| Rating | Points | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds** | 4 | RT60 target is justified by program (speech-primary, music-primary, or mixed) with reference to published ranges. Hand calculation using Sabine's equation is complete and accurate at ≥ 3 octave bands (500 Hz, 1k Hz, 2k Hz minimum). Pachyderm result is within ±0.2 s of hand calc or discrepancy is explained. Student identifies the dominant absorptive surface in the room. |
| **Mastery** | 3 | RT60 target is stated with reasonable justification. Hand calculation is complete at ≥ 2 octave bands with minor arithmetic errors. Pachyderm run is submitted. Discrepancy between methods is acknowledged even if not fully resolved. |
| **Near** | 2 | RT60 target is stated but not justified by program. Hand calculation is incomplete (one band only, or missing material areas). Pachyderm screenshot is submitted but not discussed. |
| **Below** | 1 | RT60 target is absent or incorrect by > 0.5 s. No hand calculation or calculation shows fundamental errors in applying Sabine's equation. No Pachyderm output submitted. |
| **No Evidence** | 0 | No RT60 work of any kind submitted — no hand calculation, no script/Pachyderm output, no target. |

## Criterion 3 — Material Strategy
**NAAB SC.4B** · *4 points*

> Are absorptive, reflective, and diffusive surfaces intentionally placed? Are NRC values cited from published sources? Are material choices appropriate to each zone of the hall (stage, house, back-of-house)?

| Rating | Points | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds** | 4 | Material schedule lists ≥ 5 distinct assemblies with NRC values cited from MEEB tables or manufacturer data. Each surface type is tagged on the plan or section with a clear legend. Placement logic is explained (e.g., "rear wall is diffusive to prevent flutter echo; ceiling cloud is reflective to direct early energy"). At least one material decision has been revised from an earlier draft based on RT60 feedback. |
| **Mastery** | 3 | Material schedule lists ≥ 3 assemblies with NRC values. Most surfaces are tagged on drawings. Placement rationale is present for the primary absorptive surface. Minor gaps in coverage or citation. |
| **Near** | 2 | Materials are named but NRC values are absent or estimated without citation. Drawings show finish materials but no acoustic distinction between absorptive, reflective, and diffusive roles. |
| **Below** | 1 | No material schedule submitted. Finishes on drawings are generic (e.g., "concrete," "carpet") with no NRC values and no acoustic reasoning. |
| **No Evidence** | 0 | No materials addressed anywhere — no schedule, no finishes noted, no NRC data. |

## Criterion 4 — Noise Isolation (STC)
**NAAB SC.4E** · *4 points*

> Does the student identify the critical noise partition and specify an appropriate STC wall assembly? Is there evidence of understanding mass law, flanking paths, and entry vestibule strategy?

| Rating | Points | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds** | 4 | Critical partition is correctly identified (e.g., back-of-house / loading dock wall, mechanical room boundary). STC target is derived from the NC criterion of the hall and the expected source level, with calculation shown. Assembly is specified with component layers and their individual STC contribution noted. At least one flanking path is identified and addressed (structure-borne or HVAC duct). Entry vestibule or acoustic door seal strategy is included. |
| **Mastery** | 3 | Critical partition is identified with a reasonable STC target. Assembly is specified (stud type, layer count, decoupling) without full derivation. Flanking paths are acknowledged. Vestibule or door seal is mentioned. Minor gaps in derivation or specification. |
| **Near** | 2 | A partition is identified but STC target is stated without derivation. Assembly is generic (e.g., "double drywall") without layer specification. Flanking and entry sealing not addressed. |
| **Below** | 1 | No noise isolation strategy submitted, or strategy is limited to a note such as "use thick walls" without specification or calculation. |
| **No Evidence** | 0 | Noise isolation is not addressed anywhere in the submission. |

## Criterion 5 — Decision Log Quality
**NAAB SC.4B** · *4 points*

> Do the Act I log entries demonstrate that design decisions were driven by acoustic reasoning, not just preference? Is there evidence of iteration — a decision revisited, a calculation that changed the design?

| Rating | Points | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds** | 4 | ≥ 4 substantive entries covering Weeks 1–3. At least one entry references a specific calculation or published criterion (e.g., "lowered rear wall absorptivity after RT60 came in at 1.2 s, below the 1.6–1.8 s target for mixed program"). At least one entry acknowledges a trade-off with a downstream system (daylight, structure, or circulation). Reasoning is written in the student's own voice and goes beyond description of what was done. |
| **Mastery** | 3 | ≥ 3 entries covering Weeks 1–3. At least one entry connects a decision to a calculation or criterion. Trade-off field is completed on most entries. Reasoning is present but may be thin on one or two entries. |
| **Near** | 2 | 2 entries submitted, or entries are present but reasoning fields are brief and descriptive ("I chose vineyard because it looked good"). Calculations are not referenced in the log. Trade-off field is left blank on most entries. |
| **Below** | 1 | 0 or 1 entry submitted, or entries are placeholder text. No connection between log content and acoustic work submitted in the deliverable package. |
| **No Evidence** | 0 | No Decision Log entries submitted for Act I. |
